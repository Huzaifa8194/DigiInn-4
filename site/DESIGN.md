# DigiInn360 — Design record

## Brief
Adapt the supplied Astro ONYX template for DigiInn360. Preserve oversized Archivo typography, navy/paper contrast, mono labels, square buttons, editorial whitespace and a sculptural hero. English, local preview only. Audience: businesses seeking security, software or training. Primary conversion: direct conversation.
Content source: https://www.digiinn360.com/ (reviewed November 5, 2026). Services, email and primary two phone numbers retained. No invented clients, credentials, projects, testimonials or outcome statistics. Original template pages preserved in template-archive; unused source assets retained.

## Three concepts
1. Name: the 360-degree perspective. Protective rings surround the business at the core. Signature: independently rotating metallic orbits. Assured and composed; screenshot is shield suspended in the rings. Originality 4, fit 5, feasibility 5, portability 2.
2. Material: packet inspection. A packet traverses a technical pipeline. Precise but potentially too dashboard-like. Signature: packet trace. Scores 3/4/4/4.
3. Process: closing the gaps. Open frames lock around sections as risks are addressed. Reassuring but less sculptural than the reference. Scores 4/4/4/3.
Pick 1: derives from the name and accommodates all three actual disciplines.

## System
Ground #0a1020; paper #eef0f2; muted #9aa9be; dark muted #596575; contact field #bbcedd; accent #91d0e6.
Archivo supplied local variable font for display/body; Martian local mono for labels. Display tracking -.045 to -.055em, 0.96–1.02 leading. Body 16px / 1.5. Desktop gutters 3.5–6%, phone 6%. Square panels; round rings only.

Mobile: single column hero, sculpture below actions; full-width expertise rows; stacked service detail.
Genericness: no hacker stock photos, status dashboard, made-up threat metrics, feature-card grid or neon green. The reference's sculpture becomes an original procedural security object.

## Implementation and assumptions
Existing Astro + Three.js + GSAP. All copy is server-rendered. No loader. SVG fallback for reduced motion/no WebGL, visible text if scripting fails. Pause control; offscreen/tab visibility suspension; DPR capped at 1.5. Inner pages intentionally calm. Contact is mailto/tel, no unconfigured form or claims of submission. Wordmark is a typographic interpretation, pending brand approval. Sculpture is illustrative, not live monitoring. No backend or deployment requested.

## Narrative sculpture revision
The abstract orbital shield is replaced by a six-leaf machined security enclosure. Opening reveals three server modules, four endpoints and physical circuit traces. An amber malicious packet approaches the exposed endpoint; three firewall panels insert across its path and the packet collapses at impact. An identity lock seats its shackle; the endpoint turns cyan, and the shell closes. Scroll controls all mechanisms through one reversible driver. Captions follow mechanism stages. This is an illustrative explanation, not a live security visualization.
